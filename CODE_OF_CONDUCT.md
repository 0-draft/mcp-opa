# Code of Conduct

mcp-opa-authz follows the [CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

## Summary

This community is made up of people from many different backgrounds and experiences. We are committed to keeping it open, inclusive and welcoming, so that everyone feels comfortable and safe participating.

- **Be inclusive**: people of all backgrounds, identities and experiences are welcome here
- **Be respectful**: treat others with kindness
- **Be collaborative**: work together and help each other
- **Be considerate**: your work affects others, and theirs affects you
- **Be careful with words**: harassment and exclusionary behaviour are not acceptable

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to <kanywst12@gmail.com>. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers will respond as promptly as circumstances allow.
